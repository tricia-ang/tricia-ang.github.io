About Me

My interest in technology started early, during my polytechnic years where I studied Business Informatics and worked on e-commerce platforms and software development projects involving web technologies and system design.

I later completed a Bachelor of Business in Banking and Finance,which strengthened my problem-solving and analytical skills and gave me a broader understanding of how organisations operate. Through this experience, I also clarified that my strengths and interests lie firmly on the technical side.

To pursue this direction fully, I am currently undertaking a Master of Computing at NUS , focusing on building practical technical skills in software development and data analytics. I enjoy understanding how business needs, user perspectives, and technology come together to shape effective products, and I'm motivated by breaking down problems and turning ideas into real-world solutions.
